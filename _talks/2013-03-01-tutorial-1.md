I was invited by the Liberatory Public Health Collective to give a virtual training on archiving websites and social media posts. I shared information about free and low-cost tools such as Webrecorder and discussed logistical and ethical issues that should be considered when archiving online content. The Liberatory Public Health Collective is a group based in Austin, TX committed to transforming what public health looks like by focusing on community building, education, and resource sharing.
