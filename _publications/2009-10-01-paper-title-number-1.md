This finding aid was collaboratively written by myself, Tess Derby, Mercedes Rodrigues Lima, Sabrina Moore, and Emily Teller while we were graduate students in the Archives and Public History MA program at New York University. We processed this collection during our course “Advanced Archival Description,” with assistance from Professor Martha Tenney, in Fall 2024.
